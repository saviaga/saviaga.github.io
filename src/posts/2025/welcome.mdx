## How to Add New Posts

1. Create a new `.mdx` file in the `src/posts/YYYY/` directory
2. Add frontmatter with title, summary, and date
3. Write your content in Markdown
4. The post will automatically appear on the blog page

## Frontmatter Fields

- **title**: The title of your post
- **summary**: A brief description (used in listings)
- **date**: Publication date in YYYY-MM-DD format
- **cover**: Optional cover image

## Example

```markdown
---
title: "Presented at CHI 2025"
summary: "Our paper on Human-Centered AI was accepted at CHI 2025"
date: "2025-03-15"
---

Your content here...
```
